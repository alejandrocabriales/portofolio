# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people hiring a remote senior engineer.

- **Recruiters** skim first. They need role, seniority, location/timezone overlap, availability and contact within seconds, and keywords that match the req.
- **Engineering managers and tech leads** read second, deeper. They open case studies and posts to judge how Manuel thinks: architecture decisions, trade-offs, measured outcomes.

Success is an interview request from either reader.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and writing site for Manuel Cabriales Toledo, Senior Full Stack Engineer (React, Next.js, Node, PostgreSQL), based in Montevideo, Uruguay, working remote. It turns the CV into evidence: selected work as case studies, a work history, a stack index, and long-form technical writing.

## Positioning

Production rigor, proven. The claim is "systems that hold up in production", and every part of the site backs it with something checkable: measured outcomes (e.g. Fusion's 40% FCP improvement), failure logs and post-mortems (the 111-failures chatbot post), and decisions explained with their trade-offs. Writing shows the thinking; numbers show the result. A neighbouring portfolio that lists the same stack cannot copy the evidence.

## Operating Context

- Readers arrive from LinkedIn, job applications, recruiter outreach, search, and shared post links; social/OG cards matter.
- Recruiters often read on phones between calls; managers read case studies and posts on desktop.
- Content lives in the repo: `src/config/site.ts` (identity, links, availability), `src/data/` (projects, experience, stack, profile), `src/content/blog/` (MDX posts with custom figure components).
- Availability is toggled in `AVAILABILITY` between job searches rather than removing UI.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 5 static site, Tailwind 4, MDX, Pagefind search, RSS, sitemap, JSON-LD, `llms.txt`, per-page OG images generated by `pnpm og` and committed. Deployed on Netlify; Vercel Analytics and Sentry present.
- Light and dark themes with a pre-paint theme script.
- **Keep it static and fast.** No heavy client JS; performance is part of the pitch.
- **English only**, site and posts.
- Unconfirmed facts are `null` and render nothing: no guessed links, no placeholder metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Manuel Cabriales Toledo (short: Manuel Cabriales). Role: Senior Full Stack & AI Engineer.
- Voice: first person, specific, plain. States what was owned and what changed, with numbers when they exist. Avoids filler ("passionate about") and claims that would fit any candidate.
- Headline claim: "Systems that hold up in production."

- Absent and must not be fabricated: testimonials, client logos, additional metrics, employer endorsements.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion.** Every claim links to a number, a case study, or a post. No number beats an invented one.
2. **Two-speed reading.** A recruiter gets the essentials in one screen; a manager can go as deep as they want without hunting.
3. **The site is a sample of the work.** Speed, accessibility and correctness are demonstrated, not described.
4. **Confidentiality is part of rigor.** Name only clients already public (Sky/MakerDAO); others stay anonymized (e.g. "a wellness spa in Spain").

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is required across both themes, including contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, reduced motion, and accessible text alternatives for blog figures.
